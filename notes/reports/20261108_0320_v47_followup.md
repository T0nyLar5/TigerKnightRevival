## 5. Walk-helper lag
- `walk_helper()` waits until the process is >= 45 s old, then does ONE `inspect()`: it finds TArray candidates in .data, tries them as GNames, then `names.build_reverse()` and `Objs.learn()` over the whole GObjects table through ReadProcessMemory (tens of thousands of small reads). The log banner says 10-40 s. Those reads, plus the attach, compete with the game's loading/spawn at exactly the time the hero appears; the heavy lag = this scan (hypothesis from code, not profiled). After it finds the Location offset the loop is 15 ms single reads/writes (cheap), hence "normal afterwards".
- Spawn teleport does NOT remove the need for the helper: the native snap-back pulls the pawn back toward the sync object position (obj+0x14/+0x20), which now simply starts at Camp1Pos instead of (0.5,-0.6); walking more than ~10 m would still be pulled back (the helper keeps it from happening). Not verified without the helper on V47.
- Trivial mitigation (not done): cache the found Location offset (and class layout) to a file the first time, skip the full scan next run; or lower `min_age` only if the hero appears earlier. Left as a note.
